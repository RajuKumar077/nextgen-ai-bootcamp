3. PO APPROVAL LIMITS
3.1 POs up to ₹50,000 are approved by the Line Manager.

3.2 POs above ₹50,000 and up to ₹5,00,000 are approved by the Department Head.

3.3 POs above ₹5,00,000 require Director approval.
