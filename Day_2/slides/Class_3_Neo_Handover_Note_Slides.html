Context: your company's rules

Neo has never worked at your company.

It doesn't know your limits, your approvers or your policies.

If it matters, write it down.

Rule 2Above ₹5,00,000 → Meera (Finance Controller)
Rule 3Otherwise → AP team
This is where your business knowledge matters most.

Rules: what Neo must never do

🙅

No guessing

Missing → "Not stated"

✂️

Keep it short

Max 20 words per line

🚫

No promises

Never commit to a payment date

Rules are what stop the bluffing we saw yesterday.

Neo›Class 3 · 2 · Five partsNotebookCell 2

The note, as Neo sees it

ROLE     You are an SAP procurement analyst.
TASK     Summarise this invoice dispute email.
CONTEXT  ₹5 lakh = ₹5,00,000. ABOVE ₹5,00,000 → escalate to the Finance Controller. Otherwise → AP team.
FORMAT   Vendor · Invoice number · PO number · Invoice amount · Issue · Escalate? (Yes/No + reason) · Next action
RULES    If a value is not in the email, write "Not stated". Do not guess. Max 20 words per line.
EMAIL    <<< …Rahul's email… >>>

<<< >>> shows where your note ends and the email starts. It matters after lunch.

!Run A: Escalate? No: “below ₹5 lakh”

Exact: "₹5,00,000. Above → Meera"
✓Escalate? Yes (Amount > ₹5,00,000), on every run
