Handover note›Class 2.1 · 2 · Five partsDay 2 · Morning

A good handover note

✎ Handover note
RoleYou're covering for me as AP lead.
TaskSummarise every vendor dispute email.
ContextAnything above ₹5,00,000 goes to Meera (Finance Controller).
FormatVendor · Invoice · PO · Amount · Escalate? · Next step
RulesNever promise a payment date. If something isn't in the email, write "Not stated".

Five short lines. Ravi now knows who he is, what to do, the rules, how to report and what never to do.

Read the note aloud. Everyone has written one like this.

The 5 parts: R-T-C-F-R

PartQuestion it answersForget it and…
RoleWho are you while I'm away?The tone and level of detail are off
TaskWhat exactly should you do?Ravi does something "helpful", but not that
ContextWhat rules apply here?He guesses your company's rules
FormatHow should the result look?Every answer looks different
RulesWhat must you never do?He promises things or makes things up
Reveal row by row.
Handover note›Class 2.1 · 2 · Five partsNotebookCell 2

The same note, written for the AI

ROLE     You are an SAP procurement analyst.
TASK     Summarise this invoice dispute email.
CONTEXT  ₹5 lakh = ₹5,00,000. ABOVE ₹5,00,000 → escalate to the Finance Controller. Otherwise → AP team.
FORMAT   Vendor · Invoice number · PO number · Invoice amount · Issue · Escalate? (Yes/No + reason) · Next action
RULES    If a value is not in the email, write "Not stated". Do not guess. Max 20 words per line.
EMAIL    <<< …Rahul's email… >>>

The <<< >>> marks show where your note ends and the email begins. That matters after lunch.

Examples and readers

Show, and aim

Section divider.
Handover note›Class 2.1 · 4 · ExamplesDay 2 · Morning
In real life
📎

Instead of explaining your filing system, you show Ravi three emails you already filed.

He gets it instantly.

With the AI
🧩

Give the AI three examples with the right label.

This is called few-shot. Zero examples is zero-shot.

Handover note›Class 2.1 · 4 · ExamplesDay 2 · Morning

Careful with email D

!"Please update our bank details for future payments; new letter attached."

The label is right. But this is the most common payment scam.

Ravi should call the vendor back on a known number, never change bank details from an email. Same for the AI.

Ask the room first. Bridge to the scam section in the afternoon.
