NOVA INDUSTRIES · TRAVEL POLICY (MOCK, FOR TRAINING ONLY)
Version 3.0 · Effective 01 January 2026 · Owner: HR Operations

1. BOOKING
1.1 Employees book economy class flights and AC 2-tier train tickets through the company travel desk.

1.2 Tickets must be booked at least 7 days before travel. Later bookings need a written reason from the traveller.

2. ALLOWANCES
2.1 Domestic daily allowance: ₹3,000 per day in metro cities and ₹2,000 per day in other cities.

2.2 International daily allowance: Singapore SGD 120 per day, United Kingdom GBP 100 per day, United States USD 110 per day.

2.3 Hotel limit: ₹8,000 per night in metro cities and ₹5,000 per night in other cities.

3. APPROVALS
3.1 Domestic trips longer than 5 days need Department Head approval.

3.2 Every international trip needs Director approval.

4. CLAIMS
4.1 Expense claims must be submitted within 10 working days of returning, with receipts for every expense above ₹500.

4.2 A travel advance of up to 50% of the estimated cost can be requested before the trip.
