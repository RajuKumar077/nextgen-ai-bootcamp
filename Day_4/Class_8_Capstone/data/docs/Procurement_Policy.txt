NOVA INDUSTRIES · PROCUREMENT POLICY (MOCK, FOR TRAINING ONLY)
Version 4.2 · Effective 01 April 2026 · Owner: Head of Procurement

1. PURPOSE
1.1 This policy applies to every purchase made by plants 1000 and 2000. It explains who may approve a purchase, how vendors are managed and how invoices are paid.

2. RAISING A PURCHASE ORDER
2.1 Every purchase must be raised as a purchase order (PO) in SAP before anything is ordered. Orders placed without a PO are not paid.

3. PO APPROVAL LIMITS
3.1 POs up to ₹1,00,000 are approved by the Line Manager.

3.2 POs above ₹1,00,000 and up to ₹10,00,000 are approved by the Department Head.

3.3 POs above ₹10,00,000 require Director approval.

3.4 Emergency POs may be approved by the Plant Head and must be regularised within 3 working days.

4. GOODS RECEIPT AND PAYMENT
4.1 An invoice is paid only after a 3-way match: the PO, the goods receipt and the invoice must agree.

4.2 Standard payment terms are 30 days from the invoice date.

4.3 Payment runs are executed every Tuesday and Friday.

5. VENDOR MANAGEMENT
5.1 New vendors require 3 quotations and a completed vendor registration form.

5.2 Changes to vendor bank details require written confirmation from the vendor AND a call-back verification by the Vendor Master team before activation.

5.3 A vendor rated C for two quarters in a row is reviewed by the Head of Procurement.

6. INVOICE DISPUTES
6.1 Disputes on invoices above ₹5,00,000 must be escalated to the Finance Controller.

6.2 A price variance above 1% between the PO and the invoice blocks the invoice for review.

6.3 Disputes must be resolved within 15 working days.
