NOVA INDUSTRIES · SAP PROCURE-TO-PAY PROCESS GUIDE (MOCK, FOR TRAINING ONLY)
Version 2.1 · Effective 01 April 2026 · Owner: SAP Process Team

1. THE FIVE STEPS
1.1 Purchase requisition (ME51N): created by the requesting department to ask for goods or services.

1.2 Purchase order (ME21N): created by Procurement after the requisition is approved.

1.3 Goods receipt (MIGO): posted by the warehouse when the goods are delivered.

1.4 Invoice verification (MIRO): posted by Accounts Payable when the vendor invoice arrives.

1.5 Payment (F110): the automatic payment run, executed every Tuesday and Friday.

2. CONTROLS
2.1 A 3-way match (PO, goods receipt, invoice) is required before payment. If the three do not agree, the invoice is blocked for review.

2.2 Accounts Payable cannot post an invoice for a PO that has no goods receipt, unless the PO is marked as a service.
